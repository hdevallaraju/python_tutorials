Main Sales Channels

Multi-channel distribution, described simply with clear business purpose.

OTAs (Online Travel Agencies)

Scale

Purpose: predictable demand at premium intent.
Description: distribution via MMT Luxe Selection, Airbnb Luxe, goIbibo, Booking.com.
Benefit: faster occupancy ramp and demand resilience.

Collaborations (Travel agents, partners & MICE)

Segments

Purpose: access qualified networks and group demand.
Description: partnerships with travel agents, luxury rental agencies, brands, and MICE companies.
Benefit: higher-value bookings and repeatable pipelines.

Booking / Reservation Portal

Direct

Purpose: grow direct bookings and protect margins.
Description: branded web experience with a fast booking flow and support.
Benefit: lower acquisition cost over time and stronger loyalty.

Campaigns (Boosting & Ads)

Demand

Purpose: generate demand quickly and consistently.
Description: ads and boosting on Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook.
Benefit: controllable pipeline that can be scaled seasonally.

Identify Leads through AI analysis (LinkedIn / Instagram)

Outbound

Purpose: create a scalable lead engine beyond inbound demand.
Description: AI-supported lead discovery and prioritization from LinkedIn/Instagram signals.
Benefit: better prospecting efficiency and improved lead quality.

Technology Stack

Core systems that enable scale, accuracy, and measurable performance.

Core Components

Scale-ready
  • PMS: operations, inventory, reservations.
  • Channel Manager: OTA distribution + rate/availability sync.
  • Booking/Reservation Engine: conversion, payment, confirmations.
  • Administration & analytics portal: reporting and decision support.
  • Tally integration: accounting accuracy and automation.

Benefit: fewer manual errors, faster operations, and measurable performance.

Pricing & AI Enablement

Efficiency
  • Dynamic Pricing: balance price-for-value while protecting profitability.
  • AI content generation: faster ad/campaign creatives with consistent brand tone.
  • AI workflows + human-in-loop: reliable social posting cadence with approvals.

Benefit: faster go-to-market with better consistency and control.
